# Site page /site/[id]

Scope: the per-site deep dive, demo centerpiece. Mode: Operate (owner studies one site; judges watch a 2-minute demo).
Audience/job: independent owner deciding whether to sign; must read hidden cost and break-even first, then the evidence
(tour, before/after, plan, aerial, history, trend). Constraints: every number sourced or labelled estimate; ranges, not
promises; panels render cleanly when media fields are null.

Decision note: run as a subagent with no user channel; the assigned direction was taken unattended from PRODUCT.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site read as a county surveyor's plat: one parcel, measured from inside, above and the record. Refuses the
grey SaaS card grid with KPI tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Cool linen-grey ground, iron-gall blue-black ink, surveyor's red for money the owner pays, USGS green for
money saved, ochre for unknowns. Archivo (condensed widths for headings and figures), Geist Mono for parcel codes and
sources. Hairline linework, no shadows, no gradients. Title-block header band in ink.

STORY: Owner sees what the space costs to become theirs, whether rent can be carried, what the scan and aerial show,
and who failed here before.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Ink title block (address, suite, SF, rent, city, headline) with CoStar photo at right; directly below,
the X-Ray: total range against the budget capacity bar, then cost lines as dimension lines. Tour begins below the fold
at 1440.

FORM: candidate 4 of 7 (surveyor's plat / assessor map); seed:87fcdb0ad2596a16. Signature move: every dollar range is
drawn as a survey dimension line (low and high ticks, mid mark) on one shared scale. Raises: budget as fixed capacity
(from cassette j-card: a side must not overflow its tape); fixed column grid for cost rows (from split-flap board);
rank by weight and case, not size, in tables (from timetable slide rack); section rail showing extent (from manual tab
board).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and
every shipping raster carrying its provenance
